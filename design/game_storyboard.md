## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Sci-Fi Mystery Adventure — an exploration game set in a mysterious facility where the player must collect items and overcome obstacles to reach the final chamber.

**Storyline:**

The player begins in the Docking Bay of a mysterious facility and must explore the facility to collect eight items: an Access Card, Flashlight, Repair Kit, First Aid Kit, Power Cell, Radio, Scanner, and Override Key. The player must use these items to progress through the facility and reach the Control Room. After obtaining the Override Key, the player must enter the Security Robot Chamber and face the Security Robot, who guards the final area.


## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Docking Bay - Start Room
2. Security Hall - Item: Flashlight
3. Supply Room - Item: Repair Kit
4. Medical Lab - Item: First Aid Kit 
5. Power Room - Item: Power Cell 
6. Communications Room - Item: Radio 
7. Research Lab - Item: Scanner 
8. Control Room - Item: Override Key
9. Security Robot Chamber - Villain: Security Robot
